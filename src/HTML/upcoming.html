What's coming next

Features planned for the dog training app.

Predictive training

  • Predict how many sessions or days a dog will need to master a command
  • Base predictions on breed, age, session frequency, and past command history

Accounts and dogs

  • Save training sessions to your account and see them on any device
  • Canine Good Citizen progress saved to your account for each dog
  • Change your profile picture

Command display options

  • Choose what shows for each command: score, repetitions, duration, location, distractions, and weather

Reminders

  • Email and push notifications
  • Vaccination and health reminders

Sharing and privacy

  • Share your training plans with other users
  • Choose whether your name shows on commands you share
  • Privacy settings

Personalization

  • Language
  • Date format and theme

Your data and support

  • Export or delete your data
  • Subscription plans and renewal
  • Help, support, and feedback
